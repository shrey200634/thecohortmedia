## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the ethos of a high-craft editorial studio and cultural atelier. Built for discerning cultural operators, founders, and media directors, it balances architectural precision with organic tactile flourishes. The emotional tone is authoritative, quiet, deliberate, and gallery-grade—evoking the weight of custom letterpress stationery, fountain ink drying on cotton rag, and the architectural clarity of an open studio floor.

The visual direction converges **Warm Minimalist Editorial** with **Tactile Curatorial Craft**:
- **Canvas Atmosphere:** Pure digital white is eliminated in favor of a warm, light-trapping archival paper tone. 
- **Subtle Orthogonal Cadence:** A faint 68px drafting grid runs across the background, lending structure without digital sterility.
- **Organic Gestures:** Rigorous modern sans typography is punctuated by natural cursive fountain-pen accents, hand-drawn editorial strokes, and delicate semi-translucent washi tape tabs that mark active states and milestones.
- **Radical Content Restraint:** Visual bureaucracy—such as repetitive technical chips, heavy metadata keys, and extraneous badge systems—is stripped away to spotlight narrative flow and fluid diagrams.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the physical materials of a printmaking and design atelier: mineral inks, warm archival rag, and raw earth pigments.

- **Primary Canvas (`#FAFAF7`):** Archival gallery paper. Serves as the global backdrop. Surface variations use `#F3F2EB` for recessed wells or `#FFFFFF` for pristine floating cards.
- **Primary Ink (`#1E2D50`):** Midnight blue-black mineral ink. Applied to primary typography, core interactive elements, precise geometric borders, and prominent button states.
- **Artisanal Pipeline Accents:**
  - **Amber Gold (`#D97706`):** Signals initial inquiry, spark, ideation, and active momentum.
  - **Dusty Terracotta Rose (`#BE6464`):** Represents refinement, editorial tension, drafting, and developmental stages.
  - **Calm Sage (`#4D7C5D`):** Designates realization, final output, consensus, and sustained growth.
- **Drafting Line Neutral (`#E8E6DF`):** Used at 40–60% opacity for the ambient 68px graph grid and delicate 1px architectural dividing lines.
- **Washi Neutral (`#EBE7DC` at 70% opacity):** A low-sheen vellum tone deployed with soft fibrous edge styling for contextual tape tags and structural pinning.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples modern mid-century grotesk discipline with bespoke calligraphic counterpoints.

- **Primary Structure (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Clean, proportioned, and devoid of distracting quirks. Tight display kerning creates an authoritative editorial masthead feel, while relaxed body settings preserve comfortable long-form cadence.
- **Architectural Indexing (`Space Grotesk`):** Reserved exclusively for numeric indexes, stage markers, coordinates, and technical metadata. Never set in heavy weights; always tracked out cleanly.
- **Fountain Pen Script Layer:** Hand-written notation elements (signatures, marginalia notes, parenthetical annotations, and editorial corrections) must use a natural, fluid fountain cursive (`Epilogue` or custom SVGs in production). These accents are rendered in Midnight Ink `#1E2D50` at a slight -2° to -4° rotational bias to echo genuine penmanship.
- **Editorial Rule:** Never clutter interfaces with redundant taxonomy labels like "STATUS:", "TYPE:", or "CATEGORY:". Let typographic scale, spatial position, and color convey structural meaning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored to a 68px modular rhythm (`4.25rem` at standard base scale), directly matching the visible atmospheric grid pattern.

- **Grid Infrastructure:**
  - **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column framework with 68px gutters and 68px page margins. The content container maxes out at an uncrowded 1360px.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with 34px gutters and 42px exterior margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px margins. The 68px background grid scales to a subtle 34px subdivision to prevent visual clutter on small viewports.
- **Air & Proportion:** Space is treated as a structural material. Negative space between disparate modules defaults to `space-xl` (68px / 4.25rem). Dense, packed cards are prohibited; content clusters float cleanly inside well-defined zones of breathing room.
- **Asymmetric Composition:** Large editorial headlines align to grid columns 1 through 7, while accompanying notes and pipeline visualizations cascade through columns 8 through 12, creating an asymmetrical editorial layout reminiscent of a monograph spread.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects synthetic multi-stop drop shadows, harsh ambient glows, and heavy glassmorphism. Depth is tangible, flat, and physical—relying on layered paper, 1px drafting lines, and subtle optical shifts:

- **The Drafting Surface (Base):** Background paper (`#FAFAF7`) with a crisp, 1px ambient drafting grid in `#E8E6DF` spaced every 68px.
- **Stationery Level (Cards & Containers):** Pure white archival sheets (`#FFFFFF`) sitting directly on the canvas. Outlines are rendered as crisp 1px borders using `#1E2D50` at 10% opacity (`rgba(30, 45, 80, 0.1)`).
- **Physical Paper Lift:** When an interactive item elevates (e.g., hover states on stage cards), it uses a crisp offset shadow that mimics physical stationery: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 45, 80, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 45, 80, 0.04)`.
- **Washi Tape Anchoring:** Key sections, callouts, or active pipeline stages feature a simulated washi tape accent along the upper edge or corner: a low-opacity rectangle (`#EBE7DC` at 75% opacity, `mix-blend-mode: multiply`) with irregular 1px cut edges, grounding the digital card into the grid.

## Shapes

The shape grammar is architectural and tailored, governed by Level 1 (Soft) geometry:

- **Base Curvature:** Standard containers, input fields, interactive targets, and modules carry an understated 4px corner radius (`0.25rem`). This softens the vector grid while maintaining the crisp discipline of cut paper.
- **Inner Micro-radii:** Badges, small pills, and indicators utilize a strict 2px to 4px radius. Large, exaggerated organic bubbles or pill shapes (full round) are avoided to maintain mature craft standards.
- **Washi Cutouts & Strips:** Washi accents are rectilinear with micro-sheared or straight-cut ends (0px border radius on cuts), reinforcing the feel of hand-applied tape.
- **Line Terminals:** All standalone divider rules and diagram arrows feature flat, square-cut terminals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Midnight Ink (`#1E2D50`) solid fill with off-white gallery paper text (`#FAFAF7`). 4px border-radius, 12px vertical by 24px horizontal padding. On hover, shifts to an ink wash effect (`#2A3F6D`) with a subtle 2px upward physical paper translation.
- **Secondary / Outline:** 1px border in `#1E2D50` at 35% opacity on `#FAFAF7` background. Text in `#1E2D50`. On hover, the border becomes 100% `#1E2D50` with an archival background tint (`#F3F2EB`).
- **Editorial Text Link:** Midnight Ink underline set 3px below the baseline, accompanied by a subtle fountain-pen directional arrow glyph (`→`).

### Interactive Pipeline Diagram (Hero Feature)
- **Geometry:** A continuous horizontal proportional track connecting stages with dynamic fluid SVG curves.
- **Color Progression:** 
  - Phase 1 (Ideation & Discovery): Amber Gold (`#D97706`).
  - Phase 2 (Drafting & Synthesis): Dusty Terracotta Rose (`#BE6464`).
  - Phase 3 (Execution & Output): Calm Sage (`#4D7C5D`).
- **Motion & Pulse:** Connected nodes expand slightly on hover; progress fills animate linearly with ink-like easing (`cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`). Proportional stage widths correspond to actual weight/timeline data rather than static intervals.
- **Stage Pins:** Stages are marked by delicate, semi-translucent washi tape tabs pinned directly to the upper border of the active segment.

### Cards & Modules
- Pristine `#FFFFFF` surfaces nested over the 68px grid background.
- Subtle 1px borders in `rgba(30, 45, 80, 0.08)`.
- No dense administrative metadata; dates and order indices are set in tracked `Space Grotesk` at the top right corner.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Input Fields:** Unenclosed or bottom-ruled only (`border-bottom: 1.5px solid rgba(30, 45, 80, 0.2)`). The field rests directly on the paper canvas. Focus activates a crisp 1.5px Midnight Ink line with zero synthetic halo or glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hand-pressed character. 16px square with 2px corner radius. Checked state fills with Midnight Ink and displays a sharp, architectural inner tick mark.

### Editorial Callouts & Marginalia
- Asymmetric breakout boxes set with a subtle washi tape strip at the top-left corner (`height: 12px`, `width: 48px`, `#EBE7DC` at 70% opacity, -1° angle).
- Marginal notes rendered in fountain pen styling positioned in gutters adjacent to primary body copy.
