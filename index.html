THE CAPACITY TRAP

Why does creative staffing always feel so feast-or-famine?

The Dead-Stock Dilemma

Hiring full-time designers or retainer freelancers means paying for 160 hours a month even when you only have 40 hours of actual work. Idle creative benches silently burn payroll during slow release cycles.

"Why pay full salary when content output ebbs and flows?"

The Feast-or-Famine Choke

When launch week arrives or a campaign scales up, your single contractor or in-house designer hits a bottleneck. Scrambling to recruit emergency freelancers on 48h notice destroys quality and brand consistency.

"Under-capacity during spikes, idle capacity during lulls."

The Inflexible Lock-In

Traditional agencies demand rigid 6-to-12 month fixed scopes. If your strategy pivots from weekly reels to carousel design or deep-dive copy, your contracted agency team lacks the specialized skills to adapt.

"Locked into specialists you no longer need."
